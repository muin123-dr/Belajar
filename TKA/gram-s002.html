Text 1 (For questions 1 - 4)

The Komodo dragon is the largest living lizard in the world. They are found only on a few islands in Indonesia, including Komodo and Rinca. These giant reptiles can grow up to 3 meters long and weigh over 70 kilograms. Unfortunately, their population is decreasing because of habitat loss and illegal hunting. Therefore, the government has established the Komodo National Park to protect them.

1. What is the main idea of the text? (Reading: Skimming)
2. Why is the population of Komodo dragons decreasing? (Grammar: Conjunction)
3. "Therefore, the government has established the Komodo National Park..." The word 'Therefore' shows... (Grammar: Conjunction)
4. The word 'decreasing' in the text has the closest meaning to... (Vocabulary: Synonym)
Standalone Questions (Grammar & Vocab)
5. The bridge ________ by the local government in 2015. (Grammar: Passive Voice)

9. Students ________ wear their uniform every Monday. It is a strict school rule. (Grammar: Modals)
10. I have lived in this city ________ 2010. (Grammar: Present Perfect)
Text 2 (For questions 11 - 15)

Many teenagers nowadays spend too much time on their smartphones. While smartphones are useful for communication and studying, using them excessively can cause problems. It can lead to bad posture, lack of sleep, and decreased concentration in school. Therefore, parents should limit their children's screen time to ensure they stay healthy and focused.

11. Which of the following is an OPINION based on the text? (Reading: Evaluating Fact vs Opinion)
12. The word 'excessively' in the text can be replaced by... (Vocabulary: Paraphrase)
13. From the text, we can infer that... (Reading: Inferencing)
14. What is the BEST summary of the text? (Reading: Summarizing)
15. "It can lead to bad posture..." The word 'It' refers to... (Reading: Scanning/Reference)
