Text 1 (For questions 1 - 4)

A thirsty crow flew around looking for water. Finally, he found a pitcher with a little water in it. However, the neck of the pitcher was too narrow, and his beak could not reach the water. He tried to push the pitcher down, but it was too heavy. Then, he looked around and saw some pebbles. He picked up the pebbles one by one and dropped them into the pitcher. Slowly, the water level rose until it reached the top. The crow drank the water and flew away happily.

2. Why couldn't the crow drink the water at first?
3. What is the moral value of the story?
4. What would probably happen if the crow did not find the pebbles?
Text 2 (For questions 5 - 8)

Many experts suggest that reading books before bedtime is better than looking at a smartphone. The blue light emitted by phone screens can trick your brain into thinking it is still daytime. This stops the production of melatonin, a hormone that helps you sleep. On the other hand, reading a physical book distracts your mind from daily stress and relaxes your body. Therefore, establishing a habit of reading at night can significantly improve your sleep quality.

5. What is the main idea of the text?
6. From the text, it can be implied that people who play with their smartphones before bed might...
7. Why does reading a physical book help people relax?
8. The word "Therefore" in the last sentence indicates a...
Text 3 (For questions 9 - 12)

Last week, my friends and I went camping in the mountains. We were so excited that we did not check the weather forecast. When we arrived and set up our tent, the sky suddenly turned dark. An hour later, a heavy storm hit our campsite. The wind was so strong that it broke our tent pole. Rainwater flooded our sleeping bags. Shivering and wet, we ran to our car and had to sleep in it until morning. It was a disaster.

9. What can we infer about the writer's preparation for the trip?
10. Why did the writer and his friends sleep in the car?
11. How did the writer probably feel at the end of the trip?
12. What is the best lesson we can learn from this recount text?
Text 4 (For questions 13 - 16)

Electric vehicles (EVs) are becoming very popular. Unlike traditional gas-powered cars, EVs do not use gasoline, which means they produce zero emissions and are much better for the environment. They also run very quietly. However, traditional cars still have some advantages. Refueling a gas car only takes a few minutes, whereas fully charging an EV can take several hours. Furthermore, gas stations are much easier to find on highways compared to EV charging stations.

13. The word "Unlike" in the second sentence shows that the author is going to...
14. Based on the text, what is a major drawback (disadvantage) of electric vehicles?
15. From the text, we can infer that if someone wants to go on a very long road trip across the country today, they might prefer...
16. The author wrote this text to...
Text 5 (For questions 17 - 20)

While playing football inside the house, Leo accidentally kicked the ball and broke his mother's favorite vase. Panicking, he quickly swept the broken pieces under the sofa. When his mother came home, she noticed the vase was missing and asked Leo about it. At first, Leo wanted to lie, but seeing his mother's sad face, he took a deep breath. He confessed what he had done and apologized. Instead of yelling, his mother hugged him. "Thank you for telling the truth, Leo. Things can be replaced, but honesty is priceless," she said.

17. Why did Leo sweep the broken pieces under the sofa at first?
18. Based on her reaction, what kind of person is Leo's mother?
19. What will Leo probably do if he makes a mistake again in the future?
20. What is the implied message of the story?
