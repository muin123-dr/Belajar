TEKS 1 — The Legend of Bawang Putih and Bawang Merah

Once upon a time, a kind and hardworking girl named Bawang Putih lived with her stepmother and stepsister, Bawang Merah. They were very lazy and treated Bawang Putih like a servant. One day, while washing clothes in the river, Bawang Putih accidentally lost her stepmother's scarf. She followed the river and met an old woman who had found it. The old woman returned the scarf and gave Bawang Putih a small pumpkin as a gift for helping her clean her house.

When Bawang Putih opened the pumpkin at home, it was filled with gold and jewels. Seeing this, the greedy stepmother and Bawang Merah planned to do the same. Bawang Merah purposely let a scarf float away, met the old woman, but refused to help her clean. When offered a pumpkin, Bawang Merah arrogantly demanded the biggest one. However, when she opened it at home, snakes and scorpions crawled out. They realized their mistake and apologized to Bawang Putih.

1. Which of the following outlines shows the correct main points of the story?

Pembahasan: Opsi B adalah satu-satunya ringkasan yang alurnya tepat dari awal hingga akhir sesuai teks. Bawang Putih baik dan mendapat emas, sedangkan Merah sengaja menghanyutkan selendang, bersikap sombong, dan mendapat ular.

2. Why did Bawang Merah get snakes and scorpions inside her pumpkin?

Pembahasan: Teks secara jelas menyebutkan bahwa Bawang Merah menolak membantu (refused to help) dan dengan sombong meminta labu terbesar (arrogantly demanded). Sifat buruk inilah yang membuatnya mendapat ular.

4. What does the word "demanded" in the text mean?

Pembahasan: "Demanded" berarti menuntut atau meminta dengan paksa (Asked for something forcefully), sesuai dengan sikap arogan Bawang Merah.

Pembahasan: Pesan moral dari Narrative text selalu positif. (A) Keserakahan membawa bencana (terbukti dari nasib Merah), dan (B) Kerja keras dan kebaikan akan berbuah manis (terbukti dari nasib Putih).

TEKS 3 — Discovering Raja Ampat

Raja Ampat, located off the northwest tip of Papua, Indonesia, is a spectacular destination known for its stunning marine biodiversity. While many tourists visit for the beautiful karst islands in Piaynemo, the underwater world is the real magnet for nature lovers. From vibrant coral reefs to rare sea creatures, Raja Ampat is truly a diver's paradise.

Beneath the surface, the ocean is teeming with life. Divers can encounter manta rays gliding gracefully, schools of colorful fish, and unique species like the wobbegong shark. The clear turquoise water makes it easy to explore this underwater magic. Besides nature, the local Papuan people are very welcoming. They work hard to protect their environment through conservation efforts so that future generations can still enjoy this natural wonder.

TEKS 4 — Volunteering at an Animal Shelter

Last month, I had the opportunity to volunteer at "Paws & Claws", a local animal shelter. It was my first time working directly with abandoned animals, and I was both nervous and excited. On my first day, the manager welcomed me and explained my tasks. I had to clean the cages, refill the water bowls, and feed the cats and dogs. The first few days were exhausting because I had to wake up early and do a lot of physical work. However, seeing the animals wag their tails made it all worth it.

In the second week, I faced an unexpected situation. A new rescue dog arrived very sick. I quickly assisted the shelter's vet by holding the dog gently while he gave it medicine. I learned how to stay calm during emergencies. At the end of my program, the staff thanked me for my hard work. I felt proud and learned a lot about responsibility. I definitely plan to volunteer there again next summer.

14. During the volunteering program, what did the writer do on the first day?

Pembahasan: Di paragraf pertama tertulis jelas tugas hari pertama: "I had to clean the cages, refill the water bowls, and feed the cats and dogs." Membantu dokter hewan (A) terjadi di minggu kedua.

Pilih lebih dari satu jawaban benar

Pembahasan: Penulis rela bangun pagi melakukan kerja fisik (hardworking & responsible), peduli pada hewan (caring), dan belajar tenang saat darurat (willing to learn).

16. What will the writer most likely do after finishing the program?

Pembahasan: Di kalimat paling akhir teks, penulis secara eksplisit menyatakan niatnya: "I definitely plan to volunteer there again next summer."

TEKS 5 — The Danger of Junk Food for Teens

In today's fast-paced world, junk food has become a daily staple for many teenagers. While it is delicious and easy to find, it brings serious health problems. I strongly believe that teenagers consume too much junk food, and we must limit it before it damages their future health.

First, junk food is the leading cause of teen obesity. These foods are extremely high in calories, sugar, and bad fats, but very low in essential nutrients. When teens eat this regularly without exercising, they gain weight rapidly. Second, a poor diet affects brain function. A lack of vitamins makes teenagers feel sluggish, tired, and hard to focus in class, which directly drops their academic grades.

Finally, consuming fast food constantly increases the long-term risk of dangerous diseases like diabetes and heart problems, even at a young age. In conclusion, although junk food is tasty, its harm to the physical and mental health of teenagers is much greater. Schools should provide healthier meals, and parents must guide teens to eat balanced diets.
